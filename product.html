BBQ • 4-5 Hours

2. Hexagonal Sawdust Briquettes (Ogatan)

Extruded from compressed clean sawdust with a central aeration hole. Designed for continuous smokeless high-temperature grilling in Japanese yakitori and Korean BBQ houses.

Dimensions: Hexagonal diameter 45-50mm, length 100-400mm
Heat Output: 7,200 - 7,400 kcal/kg • Ash: <3.5% natural grey
View BBQ Ogatan Specifications
75–82% Carbon • HS 4402.20

3. Natural Coconut Shell Charcoal (Arang Batok)

100% natural carbonized coconut shells. Screened curved flakes and coarse chunks. Fixed carbon 75–82%, ash <3%, calorific energy >7,200 kcal/kg. Premier industrial feedstock for rotary activation kilns, metallurgy smelting, and commercial grilling.

Raw Material: 100% Mature Indonesian Coconut Shells (Cocos nucifera)
Key Parameters: Fixed Carbon 75–82%, Ash <3%, Moisture <8%, GCV >7,200 kcal/kg
View Coconut Shell Charcoal Specs
Iodine 900 - 1,150+ mg/g

4. Coconut Shell Activated Carbon (Carbon Active)

Steam-activated granular (GAC) & powdered (PAC) coconut shell carbon. High microporosity (1,000–1,250 m²/g), superior hardness (>98%), and low ash. Ideal for gold recovery (CIP/CIL), municipal water treatment, and air purification.

Mesh Sizes: Mesh 4x8 (Gold Grade), 6x12 (Gas), 8x30 (Water), 12x40, PAC 200
Packaging: 25kg PP bags with PE liner or 500kg/1,000kg Jumbo Bags (FIBC)
