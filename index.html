About Ruma Charcoal

Engineering Indonesia's Purest Coconut Charcoal for World-Class Lounges & Brands.

PT. Bumi Ruma Nusantara (Ruma Charcoal) is an integrated Indonesian charcoal manufacturing enterprise located in Sukabumi, West Java. With over a decade of operational excellence, we produce and export over 350 metric tons monthly of high-density coconut shell briquettes and legal biomass fuels.

We operate proprietary hydraulic extrusion presses generating over 150 bar of pressure, paired with automated rotary baking ovens that guarantee uniform moisture control below 5%. The result is a crack-free briquette that delivers 2.5+ hours of odorless, sparkless, pure white ash heat.

Under the mandatory 2026 IMDG 42-24 UN 1361 Class 4.2 maritime code, our compliance desk guarantees zero shipping delays with turnkey weathering surveys, container vanning inspections, and EUDR GPS farm plot records.
