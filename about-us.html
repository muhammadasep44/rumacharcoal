Our Heritage • PT. Bumi Ruma Nusantara

Over a Decade of Engineering Integrity in Indonesian Biomass.

Production Engineering

Integrated Factory Tour: From Raw Shell to Export Container

A step-by-step walkthrough of how PT. Bumi Ruma Nusantara processes raw agricultural coconut shells into world-class export briquettes.

01

Raw Shell Sorting & Pyrolysis

We procure only aged, thick-walled mature coconut shells from registered cooperatives in Sumatra and West Java. De-silication and impurity removal ensure a pure carbon foundation.

02

Micro-Pulverization & Sifting

Industrial disc mills crush carbonized charcoal into an ultra-fine 80-100 mesh flour. Multi-stage rotary vibratory sieves eliminate foreign matter and oversized particulates.

03

Food-Grade Starch Homogenization

Pure charcoal powder is blended with exactly 3-5% organic Indonesian cassava starch and purified water. Absolutely 0% chemical accelerants, sulfur, or limestone binders are used.

04

150+ Bar Hydraulic Extrusion

High-tonnage hydraulic presses compress the paste through precision hardened tungsten dies into dense continuous logs, sliced cleanly into 25mm, 26mm, or 28mm cubes.

05

48-Hour Thermostatic Oven Baking

Briquettes enter multi-tier circulating hot air ovens operating at 80°C–90°C for two full days. Controlled dehydration extracts internal moisture strictly below 5.0%.

06

14-Day Aeration & Packing

Mandatory indoor weathering in ventilated hangars achieves full ambient thermal stability before sealed plastic wrapping, retail box packaging, and shipping container vanning.

Quality Assurance Desk

In-House Proximate Analysis Laboratory & ASTM Standards.

We do not guess quality; we measure it on calibrated laboratory instruments. Every single production batch undergoes standardized testing according to ASTM International testing procedures.

Moisture Analyzer (ASTM D2867)

Guaranteed < 5.0% moisture content preventing internal dampness, quick extinction, and foul steam.

Muffle Furnace Ash Assay (ASTM D2866)

1,000°C complete burn validation ensuring < 1.8% super-white, non-flying ash structure.

Drop-Shatter Hardness Test

Repeated 3x drops from a height of 1.5 meters onto a reinforced solid steel floor with zero cracking.

Maritime Compliance

Certified IMDG Code 42-24 UN 1361 Class 4.2 Safe Ocean Shipping

Major shipping lines enforce stringent carrier acceptance rules for carbon products. Here is how our engineering protocols prevent port holds and guarantee vessel space.

14-Day Aeration Protocol

Freshly baked briquettes complete mandatory indoor weathering in our specialized aeration hangars. This fully dissipates internal exothermic potential before container stuffing.

Accredited SHT Certification

Every shipment undergoes the official Self-Heating Test (SHT) conducted by accredited surveyors (Sucofindo / Carsurin), issuing official Negative declarations for carrier approval.

Vanning & Temperature Log

Standard 20ft & 40ft containers are inspected for watertight integrity, fitted with hanging silica desiccants, stowed according to strict weight distributions, and temperature logged.
