Our Heritage • PT. Bumi Ruma Nusantara

Over a Decade of Engineering Integrity in Indonesian Biomass.

Founded in Sukabumi, West Java, PT. Bumi Ruma Nusantara (Ruma Charcoal) began with a clear engineering mission: to eliminate common industry defects like smoking, sparking, chemical odors, and cracking in shisha briquettes by applying rigorous manufacturing standards.

Today, our modern industrial complex in Sukabumi operates high-pressure hydraulic extrusion lines, automatic rotary baking chambers, and dedicated weathering hangars. We handle the entire value chain from aged raw coconut shell carbonization to custom retail box packaging.

Under the direction of Muhammad Asep Komarudin, our export desk works directly with overseas brand owners, shisha lounge chains, and supermarket distributors to deliver consistent batches that exceed international commercial expectations.

150+ Bar Hydraulic Extrusion

Industrial hydraulic compression compacts coconut powder to extreme structural density. This guarantees briquettes remain crack-free on heated burners.

Thermostatic Rotary Ovens

48-hour continuous controlled baking extracts residual moisture down to strictly <5.0%. This prevents internal dampness and foul steam during lighting.

IMDG 42-24 Weathering Bays

Dedicated indoor aeration hangars ensure all freshly carbonized materials complete mandatory 14-day weathering before container stuffing and survey issuance.
