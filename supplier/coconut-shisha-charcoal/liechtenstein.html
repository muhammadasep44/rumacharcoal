Factory Engineering Process

From Raw Aged Shells to Master Corrugated Shipping Cartons

We operate an integrated facility in Sukabumi, West Java. No shortcuts, no outsourced blending, no sawdust adulteration.

01

Aged Shell Sourcing

Only 100% fully matured coconut shells harvested from smallholder cooperatives. Matured shells have thick lignocellulose cell walls that ensure high fixed carbon and minimal natural ash.

02

Micro-Milling (80-100 Mesh)

Raw charcoal is pulverized through rotary hammer mills and filtered through fine 80-100 mesh screens to eliminate coarse particles, sand, and impurities, guaranteeing a velvety smooth briquette grain.

03

150+ Bar Hydraulic Press

Mixed with organic food-grade tapioca starch and compressed in high-tonnage hydraulic extruders generating over 150 bar of pressure. This extreme compaction eliminates internal micro-voids and prevents cracking.

04

Rotary Thermostatic Baking

Briquetting lines feed into thermostatic drying ovens for 48 hours at controlled temperatures, curing the tapioca binder and reducing total moisture below 5.0% before entering dry weathering rooms.

Quality Assurance Desk

Zero-Crack Guarantee & Muffle Furnace Ash Testing

Cheap shisha briquette suppliers use unripe raw shells or wood sawdust, which causes briquettes to crack, crumble, or emit foul sulfur odors when placed on shisha bowls. At Ruma Charcoal, every production batch must pass three strict laboratory verification gates before container stuffing:

1

1.5-Meter Drop Shatter Evaluation

Randomly selected cubes from each extrusion run are dropped 5 consecutive times onto a steel-reinforced concrete floor. Any batch showing fracturing or crumbling is immediately rejected and recycled.

2

Muffle Furnace Ash Assays (850°C)

Samples are incinerated in our thermostatic muffle furnace according to ASTM D3174. Ash weight is measured on high-precision analytical scales (±0.0001g) to certify ash stays below 1.8%.

3

Sensory Olfactory Smoking Chamber

Cubes are ignited inside a sealed sensory evaluation chamber. Our QC engineers verify zero spark emission, zero acrid smoke, and total absence of chemical solvent aroma.

How do you handle the 2026 IMDG 42-24 Dangerous Goods shipping rules?

Under the mandatory 2026 IMDG Code 42-24, all coconut charcoal shipping requires Class 4.2 Dangerous Goods (UN 1361) protocols.
