Procurement FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for custom private label packaging?

The MOQ for custom-printed inner boxes and master cartons is one 20ft container (approximately 1,800 to 1,900 boxes of 1kg / 10kg master cartons).

Can you assist with box artwork design and dielines?

Yes. We supply free dieline vector templates (AI and PDF) for 1kg, 2kg, and 10kg boxes, and our prepress team checks your files for print-readiness free of charge.

Can I produce two different brands or box designs in one container?

Yes. You can split a 20ft container into two brands (approx. 900 cartons each) or a 40ft container into multiple brands, provided minimum printing plate runs are met.
