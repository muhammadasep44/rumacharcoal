Manufacturing Architecture

The 4 Stages of Indonesian Sawdust Ogatan Production

100% natural sawdust compressed through high-temperature screw extruders where natural wood lignin melts to bind the briquette without added glue.

01

Sawdust Sieving & Drying

Raw tropical hardwood sawdust (Meranti, Mahoni, Teak) is sieved to remove bark and stones, then flash-dried through pneumatic rotary ducts to drop moisture under 8%.

02

Thermal Lignin Extrusion

Screw extruders operating at 300°C–350°C compress the sawdust under immense friction. High heat plasticizes the wood’s natural lignin into an ultra-dense hollow hexagonal log.

03

10-Day Brick Kiln Baking

Dense wood logs are stacked inside traditional vaulted masonry kilns for 7–10 days of anaerobic carbonization, reaching temperatures of 850°C to convert wood cellulose into 85% pure carbon.

04

Cooling & SHT Weathering

Kilns are sealed airtight to cool gradually over 5 days. Charcoal undergoes mandatory IMDG weathering surveys to ensure total thermal stability prior to container stuffing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Sawdust Ogatan Procurement & Shipping FAQ

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for Sawdust Ogatan?

Our standard MOQ is one 20ft FCL (17.5 to 18.5 MT). However, we strongly recommend a 40ft High Cube container (25.5 to 26.5 MT) to minimize the ocean freight cost per metric ton delivered.

Can we order mixed containers with coconut shisha charcoal?

Yes. We frequently load mixed containers (e.g. 50% Coconut Shisha Cubes + 50% Hexagonal Sawdust Ogatan) for global distributors supplying both hookah lounges and barbecue restaurants in their territory.

How do you guarantee sawdust charcoal won't break during ocean shipping?

Our briquettes are baked for 10 full days to achieve rock-solid mechanical density. Master export cartons are constructed from heavy 5-ply double-wall kraft board, tightly hand-stowed inside containers with silica desiccants. Breakage rates upon destination arrival are typically under 1.5%.
