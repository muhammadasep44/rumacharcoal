Factory Protocol

4-Stage Precision Manufacturing

Strict engineering controls from raw material harvesting to final export packaging.

01

Feedstock Selection

Selected from mature Indonesian coconut shells with ultra-dense lignocellulosic cellular architecture.

02

High-Temp Carbonization

Carbonized under oxygen-depleted atmospheres into hard char with low volatile matter and clean pore structures.

03

Steam Rotary Activation

Injected with superheated steam (900°C - 1,000°C) in rotary kilns, carving out vast networks of micropores (<2 nm).

04

Screening & De-dusting

Precision vibratory sifting into exact Tyler mesh sizes, passing through air separators to eliminate microscopic fines.

Procurement FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Iodine 900 and Iodine 1100?

Iodine value reflects the internal micropore surface area (m²/g). Iodine 900 is ideal for standard municipal drinking water and swimming pools, while Iodine 1100+ is required for industrial gold recovery, beverage polishing, and VOC gas purification.

Do you provide acid-washed activated carbon?

Yes. We offer hydrochloric and hydrofluoric acid-washed activated carbon with ash content reduced below 1.0% and neutral pH, suitable for hemodialysis and semiconductor ultrapure water systems.

What packaging formats are available for bulk shipment?

Standard export packaging is 25 kg multi-wall craft or woven PP bags with PE inner liner (shrink-wrapped on pallets), or 500kg/550kg bulk jumbo bags.
