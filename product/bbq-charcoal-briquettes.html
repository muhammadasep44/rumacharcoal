BBQ & Sawdust Charcoal FAQ

Quick factual answers for restaurant purchasing managers and commercial charcoal wholesalers.

Ogatan is made by compressing dried sawdust under extreme pressure without chemical binders, then carbonized for weeks. The central hollow core promotes laminar airflow, delivering constant, smokeless, sparkless infrared heat for 4 to 5 hours—ideal for indoor restaurant dining and open-counter grilling.

Sawdust Ogatan is hexagonal with a center hole, providing longer burn times (4-5 hours) and high steady heat preferred by commercial HoReCa kitchens. Coconut Pillow Briquettes ignite faster (15-20 minutes) and are popular for outdoor domestic BBQ, campings, and retail supermarket bags.
