Industrial Purity & Quality

Why Global Buyers Choose Our Coconut Shell Charcoal

From raw kiln carbonization to vibratory multi-deck screening, our strict factory protocols eliminate foreign impurities, stones, and uncarbonized matter.

Superior Fixed Carbon (75–82%)

Carbonized via controlled indirect drum pyrolysis. High carbon density delivers exceptional yield in activated carbon rotary kilns and superior chemical reduction efficiency in metallurgical smelting.

Double Screened & Magnet Separated

Every metric ton passes through mechanical rotary tumblers and high-gradient rare-earth magnetic bars, ensuring dust (<2mm) is under 0.2% and completely free from nails, sand, or foreign stones.

Non-DG Maritime Vanning Certified

Shipped under IMDG Code 42-24 UN 1361 regulations. Every container is inspected by independent surveyors (Carsurin / Sucofindo) with SHT (Self-Heating Test) negative certificates for rapid, smooth customs release.
