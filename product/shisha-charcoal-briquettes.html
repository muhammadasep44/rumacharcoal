Engineered Quality

How We Eliminate Cracking & High Ash (The Factory Process)

Many global shisha distributors lose clients because cheap suppliers use unripe shells, wood dust adulteration, or incomplete carbonization. At Ruma Charcoal, every batch follows 6 strict engineering controls:

Matured Thick Shells Only

Inspected for ≥ 3mm shell thickness and carbonized in brick kilns to purge heavy tars.

Fine 60-Mesh Magnetic Screening

Crushed fine char passes through neodymium magnets to remove every trace of metallic contaminants.

Pure Tapioca Binder (Zero Chemicals)

Bound exclusively with 3-5% food-grade natural starch. Zero nitrates, sulfur, or petroleum binders.

150+ Bar Hydraulic Extrusion

Extreme mechanical compaction forms high-density logs that eliminate thermal expansion cracks.

48-Hour Computerized Tunnel Drying

Progressive baking at 80°C ensures internal core moisture safely settles below 5%.

1-Meter Drop Test (3x Zero Crack)

Random batch cubes must survive 3 consecutive drops onto concrete before packaging authorization.
