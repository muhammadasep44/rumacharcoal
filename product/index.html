Natural Coconut Shell Charcoal (Arang Batok)

100% natural kiln-carbonized mature coconut shells. Screened curved flakes and coarse chunks. Fixed carbon 75–82%, ash <3%, calorific energy >7,200 kcal/kg. Premier industrial feedstock for rotary activation kilns, metallurgical smelting reduction, and gourmet barbecue.

Fixed Carbon Content 75% – 82.0% Min (ASTM D3172)
Available Sizing Natural Flakes / Chunks 3-8cm / Mesh 4x8
Gross Calorific Value 7,200+ kcal/kg High Thermal Output
Packaging Formats 25kg/50kg PP Bags / 500kg-1000kg Jumbo

Klaster 4 • HS 3802.10.00

Coconut Shell Activated Carbon (GAC & PAC)

Steam-activated granular (GAC) & powdered (PAC) carbon. High iodine value (900–1,150+ mg/g), superior ball-pan hardness (>98%), and low ash (<3%). Engineered for gold recovery (CIP/CIL circuits), municipal potable water plants, and VOC air adsorption.

Iodine Value 900 – 1,150+ mg/g (ASTM D4607)
Hardness Index ≥ 98.0% Min (Zero Gold Tailing Loss)
Available Mesh 4×8, 6×12, 8×30, 12×40, PAC 200
Packaging 25kg PP w/ Liner • 500kg/1,000kg Jumbo Bags
