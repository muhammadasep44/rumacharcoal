High-Adsorption Carbon Sizing

Industrial Mesh Grades for Specific Treatment Processes

Our coconut shell activated carbon is carbonized from mature shells and steam-activated at 900–1,100°C in rotary kilns, followed by precision multi-deck screening and magnetic destoning.

Gold Hydrometallurgy • 4×8 Mesh

Gold Grade Mesh 4×8

Particle Size: 2.36 – 4.75 mm

Specifically engineered for gold recovery circuits (CIP, CIL, CIC). High CTC activity (55–65%) and supreme attrition resistance prevent carbon attrition breakdown in agitated pulp tanks.

  • Iodine: 1,050 – 1,150+ mg/g
  • Hardness ≥ 98.5% (Zero Gold Tailing Loss)
  • Gold adsorption kinetics (R-value ≥ 65%)
Drinking Water • 8×30 Mesh

Water Grade Mesh 8×30

Particle Size: 0.60 – 2.36 mm

The worldwide benchmark fraction for municipal potable water treatment and domestic point-of-use (POU) filter cartridges. High fluid velocity with minimal bed pressure drop.

  • Iodine: 950 – 1,050 mg/g
  • Ash ≤ 2.8% • Food Grade Certified
  • Rapid chlorine, taste, odor & THMs removal
Air & Gas • 6×12 / 12×40

Vapor Grade Mesh 6×12

Particle Size: 1.70 – 3.35 mm

Engineered for vapor-phase adsorption, solvent recovery, respiratory canisters, and biogas desulfurization (H2S removal). Clean micropores trap small VOC gas molecules.

  • CTC Activity: 50% – 60%
  • Solvent recovery • Gas sweetening
  • Bulk density: 0.48 – 0.52 g/cm³
Decolorization • 200/325 Mesh

Powdered PAC Carbon

Particle Size: < 0.075 mm (Fine Powder)

Ultra-fine powdered activated carbon providing instantaneous mass transfer. Used in municipal emergency taste/odor dosing, wastewater flocculation, and sugar syrup decolorization.

  • Sugar & chemical decolorization
  • Acid-washed ultra-low ash options

Technical Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed technical specifications for procurement managers, gold metallurgists, and chemical process engineers.

Coconut shell activated carbon features a predominantly microporous internal pore distribution (<2 nm diameter) and the highest natural hardness index (>98% ball-pan hardness). In gold CIP/CIL tanks, it resists attrition and prevents soluble gold loss into tailings. In water purification, it provides enormous surface area (1,000–1,250 m²/g) to capture chlorine, THMs, and organic contaminants.
