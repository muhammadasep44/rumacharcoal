Industrial Procurement Q&A

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential clarifications on chemical purity, particle sizing, sample dispatch, and export customs compliance.

Indonesian coconut shells possess a predominantly microporous structure with an exceptionally high natural hardness index (>98%). When activated via steam at 900–1100°C, it creates extensive internal surface area (1,000–1,200 m²/g) ideal for gas-phase adsorption, gold cyanide recovery, and potable water filtration.
We produce standard industrial mesh sizes including 4x8, 6x12, 8x30, and 12x40 mesh, as well as unscreened natural raw flakes. Packaging is available in 25kg / 50kg PP woven bags or 500kg / 1,000kg heavy-duty Jumbo Bags with discharge spouts.
Raw shells undergo rigorous manual sorting before carbonization. Post-crushing, granules pass through vibrating sieve screens with forced air cyclonic separation to eliminate dust and sand, followed by dual high-intensity neodymium industrial magnets to extract any magnetic minerals.
A standard 20ft container loads approximately 16 to 17 Metric Tons, while a 40ft High Cube container loads up to 25 to 26 Metric Tons in 25kg PP bags or 500kg/1000kg Jumbo Bags, strictly adhering to shipping line gross payload and highway axle load limits.
