100% Upcycled Agricultural Biomass

Zero Tree Felling. Pure Cassava Starch Binder. Zero Chemicals.

Indonesia is the world's largest coconut producer. Rather than letting discarded shells rot in landfills or be burned openly causing air pollution, Ruma Charcoal upcycles this high-lignin biomass into premium briquettes.

Unlike synthetic commercial briquettes that use chemical accelerants, limestone, or coal dust, we use strictly 100% food-grade organic tapioca starch (3-5%) from Indonesian cassava root.

The result is a completely non-toxic combustion certified free of heavy metals (Lead <0.05 ppm, Arsenic <0.01 ppm) by SGS laboratory assays.
