100% Upcycled Agricultural Biomass

Upcycling Agricultural Waste into High-Calorie Bioenergy.

Indonesia is the world's largest coconut producer. Millions of metric tons of coconut shells are discarded annually as byproduct of coconut water, desiccated coconut, and virgin coconut oil (VCO) extraction.

Instead of allowing these massive shell volumes to rot in municipal landfills—emitting powerful methane greenhouse gases—or be burned openly, Ruma Charcoal upcycles this dense, high-lignin biomass into clean-burning briquettes.

Because coconut palms (Cocos Nucifera) produce fruits continuously for 60 to 80 years, not a single tree is cut down during harvesting. The carbon released during burning is purely biogenic and part of an active annual natural carbon cycle.

True Circular Bioeconomy

Zero reliance on virgin timber or mineral coal. 100% recovered agricultural waste streams.

Canopy Preservation

Coconut groves remain intact, preserving soil moisture, coastal biodiversity, and local microclimates.

Methane Emission Abatement

Diverting shell discard from anaerobic dumpsites prevents significant fugitive methane release.

100% Food-Grade Organic Cassava Binder: Zero Chemical Adhesives

Unlike cheap commercial briquettes bound with toxic synthetic resins, petroleum accelerants, or heavy limestone fillers, Ruma Charcoal uses purely edible botanical starch.

Ruma Charcoal Standard

100% Botanical Cassava Starch

  • Binder Origin: 100% food-grade organic tapioca starch derived from Indonesian cassava root.
  • Combustion Fumes: Odorless, neutral, zero sulfur dioxide (SO₂), and zero eye-stinging acrid fumes.
  • Heavy Metals: SGS certified: Lead (Pb) < 0.05 ppm, Arsenic (As) < 0.01 ppm.
  • Ash Quality: Pure white / light silver ash (< 1.8%) that clings together without falling into shisha bowls.
Low-Grade Conventional Briquettes

Synthetic Chemical Formulations

  • Binder Origin: Synthetic adhesives, petroleum residues, sodium nitrate accelerants, and wood glue.
  • Combustion Fumes: Pungent chemical smoke, causing headaches and altering natural hookah tobacco flavors.
  • Heavy Metals: High concentration of mineral contaminants, silica dirt, and coal dust residues.
  • Ash Quality: Dark brownish / muddy grey ash (> 5.0%) with excessive flying particles and quick crumbling.

Advanced Pyrolysis Technology

Low-Emission Retort Kilns: Recovering Liquid Smoke for Organic Agriculture.

Traditional earth-pit charcoal burning releases heavy particulate matter and volatile gases directly into the atmosphere. Our Sukabumi facility utilizes enclosed retort technology with condensation condensers.

70% Atmospheric Emission Reduction

Thermal re-combustion loops incinerate volatile gases internally, generating process heat while slashing PM2.5 emissions.

Pyroligneous Acid (Wood Vinegar) Capture

Smoke vapors are condensed and collected as natural liquid smoke (pyroligneous acid), removing air pollutants completely.

Returned to Farming Cooperatives

Refined liquid smoke is distributed free to local farming communities as an organic bio-pesticide and natural soil conditioner.

Zero-Waste Closed Loop

Nothing is discarded. From raw shell to biochar, pyroligneous fertilizer, and high-density export briquettes, our plant embodies industrial ecology in action.
